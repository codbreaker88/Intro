Anti-Cheat Policy

Our enforcement system monitors accounts for violations of the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including but not limited to:

  • Use of unauthorized third-party software
  • Modification of game files or memory
  • Teammate abuse or intentional team sabotage
  • Rank manipulation and match fixing
  • Offensive language or harassment
  • Account sharing or account trading

Confirmed violations result in warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans depending on severity and report history.
